## Overview

3–5 sentences about what this material covers and what its main idea or goal is.

## Key points

The most important claims, facts, and conclusions, as bullet points — each one complete sentence. Use as many as the content genuinely warrants: a short clip may need only 2–3, a dense talk up to ~10. Synthesise; do not restate the material sentence by sentence or pad the list to hit a count.

Rules:
- Always respond in: {output_language}, regardless of the source language.
- Do not add information not present in the source.
- Preserve the accuracy of numbers, names, and dates.
- Quoted text in quotation marks — only if that exact wording appears verbatim in the source.
- If the source contains timestamps in [MM:SS] or [HH:MM:SS] format — always include them next to the relevant key points. This lets the user jump to that moment in the video. If a key point has no timestamp in the source (e.g. web pages and PDFs have none), simply omit the marker — NEVER write a placeholder like "[Not specified]", "[Не указано]", "[N/A]" or "[—]".
- Do not write "the author says", "the video explains". Write directly: "X happens because Y".
